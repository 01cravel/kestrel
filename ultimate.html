The discipline is part of the portfolio

The rules that stop conviction becoming recklessness.

The allocation is only scientific if Kestrel records the decision before the result and applies the same rules afterwards.

  1. Enter in four equal stages.Invest 25% of each target now, then another 25% every two weeks. Finish in six weeks.
  2. Check monthly; act quarterly.News is monitored daily, but allocations are reviewed on fixed quarterly dates.
  3. Use evidence, not price alone.A fall is not a sell signal. Sell or replace when the stated business test breaks.
  4. Keep the hard ceilings.No direct company above 6%; no economic theme above 12%; true company exposure below 8%; direct stocks below 40%; Bitcoin at or below 8%; no leverage.
  5. Rebalance meaningful drift.Return to target when a holding moves over three points, or 25% relative to its target.
  6. Judge the whole portfolio.Compare after fees with VT, record maximum fall and every rule breach through 5 August 2028.
