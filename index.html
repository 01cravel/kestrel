Plain-English method

How Kestrel reaches a view

Research scoreA relative rank built from equal parts value, quality and momentum, following MSCI’s published Core Multiple-Factor structure.
Decision scoreThe research score plus a capped zero-to-five point SEC filing adjustment based on fresh buying, independent manager agreement and disclosed portfolio concentration. It never changes the Buy confidence gate.
Business healthAre sales, profits and returns healthy?
PriceDoes today’s price look sensible against earnings and growth?
DirectionIs the company improving or weakening?
AnalystsAre credible analysts broadly positive, and is their view changing?
RiskCould debt, volatility or concentration make this unsafe for your portfolio?
ConfidenceIs the evidence complete, current and independently checked?

The league score ranks research evidence; it does not predict the chance of profit. Analyst agreement is shown, while revisions and earnings surprises drive the sentiment factor. Hold is the normal answer. Missing evidence never counts as positive evidence.

Evidence before confidence

Kestrel’s source hierarchy

Every important number must keep its source, period, unit, currency and retrieval time. A model can transform evidence, but it cannot promote its own answer into a fact.

Tier 1 · AuthoritativeSarwa records, regulator filings, exchanges, issuers, central banks and government agencies establish facts.
Tier 2 · InstitutionalLicensed pricing, corporate actions, estimates and risk data validate and standardise those facts.
Tier 3 · ProvisionalFinnhub, FMP and Yahoo help with an early view, but cannot support the highest confidence by themselves.
Tier 4 · ExperimentalAlternative data and model outputs can support research but never establish a fact alone.

Missing evidence never counts as positive evidence. Conflicts lower confidence automatically.
